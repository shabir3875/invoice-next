FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you get started

What can I manage in Invoice Next?

Invoice Next brings invoices, estimates, supported business documents, customers, suppliers, products, services, expenses, payments, statements, and reports into one workspace.

Can I convert an estimate into an invoice?

Yes. Supported estimate information can be reused in an editable invoice draft so it can be reviewed before completion.

Can I record a partial payment?

The application advertises full and partial payment records and shows the remaining invoice balance.

Does Invoice Next process card payments?

This website does not claim built-in online card processing. Payment tracking refers to recording and monitoring payments.
