Before you send an invoice

Confirm the client name, invoice number, dates, currency, line items, calculations, tax treatment, payment instructions, and terms. Requirements vary by location and business type, so check the rules that apply to your transaction.

Frequently asked questions

What should this page's invoice include?

Include your business and client details, a unique invoice number, issue and due dates, itemized products or services, applicable taxes or discounts, the total due, and clear payment instructions.

Can I use Invoice Next for repeat clients?

Yes. Invoice Next is designed to keep reusable client, product, and service details organized for future documents.

Does Invoice Next process client payments?

No. Add the external payment method agreed with your client, then record received payments in your invoicing workflow.
