Benefits of Using a Self-Employed Invoice Template

A self-employed invoice template is a great option for freelancers because it can:

Save time in the long run as you reuse the same template.
Keep you organized with expenses and taxes.
Give you a professional look to match your services.
Make outlining services and deliverables easy.
Establish a springboard for growing your business.

Why Should I Use a Self-Employed Invoice Template?

Freelancer invoicing is much more than just sending an email with your payment address.

Once you get the ball rolling in this self-employed business, you need to keep up a professional front in your networking, communication, deliverables—and in your finances. A professional invoice helps build a long-term relationship.

Nearly anyone who is self-employed in any capacity benefits from a consistent invoicing process.
